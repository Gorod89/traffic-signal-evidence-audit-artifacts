NOASSERTION

`NOASSERTION` is a provenance and rights-status marker. It is not a license,
permission, public-domain dedication, fair-use determination, or waiver.

A file marked `NOASSERTION` has not received a sufficiently supported rights
determination for open redistribution. Unless an independent license notice
clearly grants the required rights, exclude the file from an open archival
deposit and provide only lawful acquisition instructions, source identifiers,
checksums, and citations.
